# IEC 60204-1 Learning Hub

## Purpose

IEC 60204-1 specifies requirements for the electrical equipment of machinery to ensure safe design, operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

## Scope

The standard addresses:

- Electrical safety
- Protection against electric shock
- Control circuits
- Protective bonding
- Emergency stop functions
- Electrical documentation
- Verification and testing

## Key Topics

### Safety Requirements
Protection against direct and indirect contact.

### Control Circuits
Design principles for machine control systems.

### Protective Bonding
Earthing and equipotential bonding requirements.

### Emergency Stop
Emergency stop devices and circuit requirements.

### Documentation
Circuit diagrams, terminal plans, and equipment identification.

### Verification
Inspection, testing, and compliance verification activities.
